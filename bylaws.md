# Bylaws of Columbia Gadget Works

*Draft v4, 9 October 2026. Not adopted. Items in [brackets] are still open.*

These bylaws hold only what Missouri law requires, what protects members, and what should be hard to change. Everything else is policy (`policies/`), which the board can change at any time.

---

## 1. Name and purpose

1.1 Columbia Gadget Works ("CGW") is a Missouri nonprofit corporation with members, under RSMo chapter 355. If these bylaws and the Articles of Incorporation differ, the Articles control.

1.2 CGW is organized exclusively for charitable, educational, religious, or scientific purposes within the meaning of Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code. It carries out that purpose by running a community workshop where people share tools, space, and skills.

1.3 As the Articles require:
- No part of CGW's net earnings benefits any member, director, officer, or other private person, except reasonable pay for services.
- No substantial part of CGW's activities is lobbying.
- CGW takes no part in any political campaign.
- CGW carries on no activity that a 501(c)(3) organization may not.

## 2. Members

2.1 **One class.** CGW has one class of voting member. A person is a member while they meet the requirements set by policy: a signed waiver and member agreement, and current dues or an approved dues waiver. Every member has the same rights, whatever they pay.

2.2 **Good standing.** A member is in good standing if they meet those requirements and are not suspended. Only members in good standing may vote or hold office.

2.3 **Ending membership.** Membership ends when a member resigns, stops paying as policy describes, or is expelled. Membership gives no ownership of CGW property and cannot be transferred.

2.4 **Expulsion or suspension.** The board may expel or suspend a member only after all of the following:
- written notice to the member, with the reasons, at least 15 days beforehand;
- a chance for the member to be heard by the board, in person or in writing, at least 5 days before the expulsion or suspension takes effect;
- a two-thirds vote of the directors in office.

2.5 **Access suspension.** When safety, property, or a person is at risk, any two directors may immediately suspend someone's access to the Space or to a tool. The suspension ends after 14 days unless the board confirms it. It does not affect the member's vote.

## 3. Board of directors

3.1 **Composition.** The board has five directors: the President, Vice President, Secretary, Treasurer, and Shop Steward. Members elect each seat separately.

3.2 **Terms.** Directors are elected at each annual meeting and serve until the next one, or until a successor is elected. Any member in good standing who is 18 or older may run. No one holds more than one seat.

3.3 **Powers.** The board manages CGW. It decides everything not reserved to the members, including policy, the budget, contracts, appointments, membership, and discipline.

3.4 **Meetings.** The board meets at least quarterly. Directors may attend by video or phone. The President or any two directors may call a special meeting on 48 hours' notice. Meetings are open to members, except closed sessions on discipline, personnel, hardship applications, or legal matters.

3.5 **Vacancies.** The board fills a vacancy by appointment until the next annual meeting. If fewer than three directors remain, they must call a member vote to fill the empty seats.

3.6 **Removal.** Members may remove a director by member vote. The board may remove a director who misses three regular meetings in a row without being excused.

3.7 **Unpaid.** Directors are not paid for board service. A director may be paid for teaching or other work on the same terms as any member.

3.8 **Conflicts of interest.** A director or appointed volunteer with a personal financial interest in a decision must disclose it and must not vote on it. The minutes record both. No one approves a payment to themselves.

## 4. Officers

4.1 The **President** chairs meetings, sets agendas, and represents CGW.

4.2 The **Vice President** acts for the President when needed.

4.3 The **Secretary** keeps the minutes, the member roster, and the official text of these bylaws and the policies. The Secretary sends notices and runs member votes.

4.4 The **Treasurer** keeps the books, prepares the budget and the annual financial report, and makes the state and IRS filings.

4.5 The **Shop Steward** looks after the shop floor. Zone Bosses report to the Shop Steward, who oversees safety, facilities, and the space between zones.

4.6 The board may assign further duties by policy.

## 5. Voting

CGW has two kinds of vote:
- **Member votes** decide who runs CGW and what the bylaws say. They happen at the annual meeting, or when the board or members call one.
- **Board votes** decide everything else, including all policy. The board can hold one at any time.

### Member votes

5.1 **What members decide.** Members:
- elect and remove directors;
- amend these bylaws;
- approve dissolution, merger, or the sale of substantially all of CGW's assets;
- decide any question the board refers to them.

Only members can amend these bylaws.

5.2 **Annual meeting.** The annual meeting is held each April, on a date the board sets. Members elect the board there, and the Treasurer reports on the year's finances.

5.3 **Calling a vote.** The board may call a member vote at any time. It must call one within 45 days if [10% of members, and at least 2,] ask in writing.

5.4 **Notice.** Between 10 and 60 days before voting opens, the Secretary emails notice to every member at the address on their member record and posts it on Discord. The notice states:
- each question, with the full text of any bylaw change;
- how and when to vote;
- the quorum;
- the share of votes needed to pass.

5.5 **Who votes.** Any member in good standing on the day voting opens may vote, with one vote each. Proxy voting is not allowed.

5.6 **Casting and recording votes.** Members vote by secret ballot, in person or online, using the method set by policy. The Secretary records the votes. A ballot stays open for at least 7 days. When there is a meeting, the ballot closes at the end of it.

5.7 **Quorum.** A vote counts only if at least 10% of eligible members, and no fewer than 5, cast a ballot. Abstentions count toward quorum.

5.8 **Passing.** A question passes by a majority of votes cast, except:
- amending these bylaws, dissolution, merger, or the sale of substantially all assets needs two-thirds of votes cast;
- in an election, the candidate with the most votes wins, and a tie goes to a runoff ballot.

5.9 **Adoption.** These bylaws replace all earlier bylaws once the members adopt them by member vote. The date and result of each adopting or amending vote are recorded in this repository's history.

### Board votes

5.10 **Policy.** The board adopts, changes, and repeals policy by majority vote, at any time. A policy takes effect when it is published, and it may not conflict with these bylaws or the Articles.

5.11 **Voting on GitHub.** Directors vote on a policy change by reviewing its pull request in CGW's governance repository:
- an approving review is a yes;
- a "request changes" review is a no.

The change passes when a majority of directors approve it. It is then merged and takes effect. The pull request and its reviews are the record of the vote.

5.12 **Voting at meetings.** Any other board decision is made at a board meeting. A majority of directors in office is a quorum, and a motion passes by majority of directors present; a tie fails. The Secretary records each vote in the minutes. At each meeting, the board also confirms the policy changes merged since the last meeting, unless every director approved them.

## 6. Delegation

6.1 The board may create zones and other roles, and delegate authority to the volunteers it appoints to them. Each delegation is set out in policy, and the board may withdraw it.

6.2 Appointed volunteers are not officers or directors. They may not sign contracts or commit CGW to ongoing costs unless the board authorizes them in writing.

## 7. Indemnification

7.1 CGW indemnifies its directors, officers, and appointed volunteers for actions taken in good faith on CGW's behalf, to the fullest extent Missouri law allows.

## 8. Dissolution

8.1 If CGW dissolves, the board first pays, or makes provision for, all of CGW's liabilities. It then distributes the remaining assets exclusively for CGW's purposes, to one or more organizations qualifying under Section 501(c)(3). Any assets not distributed this way are disposed of by the Circuit Court of the county where CGW's principal office is located, for those same purposes. This follows the Articles.
