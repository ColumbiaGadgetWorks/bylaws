# Bylaws of Columbia Gadget Works

*Draft v3, 8 October 2026. Not adopted. Items in [brackets] are still open.*

These bylaws contain only what Missouri law requires, what protects members, and what should be hard to change. Everything else is a policy (`policies/`), which the board can change more easily.

---

## 1. Name and purpose

1.1 Columbia Gadget Works ("CGW") is a Missouri nonprofit corporation with members, under RSMo chapter 355. If these bylaws and the Articles of Incorporation disagree, the Articles control.

1.2 CGW is organized exclusively for charitable, educational, religious, or scientific purposes within the meaning of Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code. It carries out that purpose by running a community workshop where people share tools, space, and skills.

1.3 As the Articles require:
- No part of CGW's net earnings benefits any member, director, officer, or other private person. CGW may pay reasonable compensation for services and reimburse documented expenses.
- No substantial part of CGW's activities is lobbying.
- CGW takes no part in any political campaign for or against a candidate.
- CGW carries on no activity that a 501(c)(3) organization may not carry on.

## 2. Members

2.1 **One class.** CGW has one class of voting member. A person is a member while they meet the requirements set by policy: signed waiver and member agreement, and current dues or an approved dues waiver. Every member has the same rights, whatever they pay.

2.2 **Good standing.** A member is in good standing if they meet those requirements and are not suspended. Only members in good standing may vote or hold office.

2.3 **Who votes.** Any member in good standing on the day voting opens may vote.

2.4 **Ending membership.** Membership ends when the member resigns, stops paying as policy describes, or is expelled. Membership gives no ownership of CGW property and cannot be transferred.

2.5 **Expulsion or suspension.** The board may expel or suspend a member only after all of the following:
- written notice to the member, with the reasons, at least 15 days before it takes effect;
- a chance for the member to be heard by the board, in person or in writing, at least 5 days before it takes effect;
- a vote for it by two-thirds of the directors in office.

2.6 **Access suspension.** When safety, property, or a person is at risk, any two directors may immediately suspend someone's access to the Space or to a tool. The suspension ends after 14 days unless the board confirms it. An access suspension does not affect the member's vote.

## 3. Member votes

3.1 **What members decide.** Members:
- elect and remove directors;
- amend these bylaws;
- approve dissolution, merger, or the sale of substantially all of CGW's assets;
- decide any question the board refers to them.

3.2 **Annual meeting.** CGW holds an annual meeting each April, on a date the board sets. Members elect the board, and the Treasurer reports on the year's finances.

3.3 **Calling a vote.** The board may call a member vote at any time. It must call one within 45 days if [10% of members, and at least 2,] ask for it in writing.

3.4 **Notice.** Between 10 and 60 days before voting opens, the Secretary emails notice to every member at the address on their member record and posts it on Discord. The notice states:
- each question, with the full text of any bylaw change;
- how and when to vote;
- the quorum;
- the share of votes each question needs to pass.

3.5 **Voting.** Members vote by secret ballot, either online or in person, as policy describes. A ballot stays open for at least 7 days. When there is a meeting, the ballot closes at the end of it. Proxy voting is not allowed.

3.6 **Quorum.** A vote counts only if at least 10% of eligible members, and never fewer than 5, cast a ballot (abstentions count toward quorum).

3.7 **Passing.** A question passes with a majority of votes cast, except:
- amending these bylaws, dissolution, merger, or selling substantially all assets needs two-thirds of votes cast;
- in elections, the candidate with the most votes wins, and a tie goes to a runoff ballot.

## 4. Board of directors

4.1 **Composition.** The board has five directors: the President, Vice President, Secretary, Treasurer, and one At-Large Director. Members elect each seat separately.

4.2 **Terms.** Directors are elected at each annual meeting and serve until the next one, or until a successor is elected. Any member in good standing who is 18 or older may run. No one holds more than one seat.

4.3 **Powers.** The board manages CGW. It adopts policy and the budget, appoints volunteers to run zones and functions, signs contracts, and handles membership and discipline. The board may not amend these bylaws.

4.4 **Meetings.** The board meets at least quarterly.
- Directors may attend by video or phone.
- The President or any two directors may call a special meeting on 48 hours' notice.
- Meetings are open to members, except closed sessions on discipline, personnel, hardship applications, or legal matters.

4.5 **Quorum and action.** Three directors are a quorum. A motion passes with a majority of directors present; a tie fails. The board may act without a meeting only if every director consents in writing. An email, or an approving review on GitHub, counts as written consent.

4.6 **Vacancies.** The board fills a vacancy by appointment until the next annual meeting. If fewer than three directors remain, they call a member vote to fill the empty seats.

4.7 **Removal.** Members may remove a director by member vote under Section 3. The board may remove a director who misses three regular meetings in a row without being excused.

4.8 **Unpaid.** Directors are not paid for board service. A director may be paid for teaching or other work on the same terms as any member.

4.9 **Conflicts of interest.** A director or appointed volunteer with a personal financial interest in a decision must disclose it and must not vote on it. The minutes record both. No one approves a payment to themselves.

## 5. Officers

5.1 The **President** chairs meetings, sets agendas, and represents CGW.

5.2 The **Vice President** acts for the President when needed.

5.3 The **Secretary** keeps the minutes and the member roster, keeps the official text of these bylaws and the policies, sends notices, and runs member votes.

5.4 The **Treasurer** keeps the books, prepares the budget and the annual financial report, and makes the state and IRS filings.

5.5 The **At-Large Director** takes on duties the board assigns.

## 6. Delegation

6.1 The board may create zones and other roles, and delegate authority to the volunteers it appoints to them. Each delegation is set out in written policy, and the board may withdraw it.

6.2 Appointed volunteers are not officers or directors. They may not sign contracts or commit CGW to ongoing costs unless the board authorizes it in writing.

## 7. Money and records

7.1 **Fiscal year.** CGW's fiscal year runs from 1 June to 31 May.

7.2 **CGW's accounts.** All funds, bank and payment accounts, domains, and online accounts are held in CGW's name. At least two directors have access to each.

7.3 **Records.** The official text of these bylaws and every policy, with its full change history, is kept in CGW's public governance repository on GitHub. The website publishes the text directly from that repository. Members may inspect CGW's records as Missouri law provides.

## 8. Indemnification

8.1 CGW indemnifies its directors, officers, and appointed volunteers for actions taken in good faith on CGW's behalf, to the fullest extent Missouri law allows.

## 9. Amendments and policy

9.1 **Bylaws.** Only the members can amend these bylaws, under Section 3.

9.2 **Policy.** The board adopts, changes, and repeals policy by majority vote. A policy takes effect when it is published. No policy may conflict with these bylaws or the Articles.

## 10. Dissolution

10.1 If CGW dissolves, the board first pays, or provides for, all of CGW's liabilities. It then distributes the remaining assets exclusively for CGW's purposes, to one or more organizations that qualify under Section 501(c)(3). Any assets not distributed this way are disposed of by the Circuit Court of the county where CGW's principal office is located, for those same purposes. This follows the Articles.

## 11. Adoption

11.1 These bylaws replace all earlier bylaws when the members adopt them.
