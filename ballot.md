# Ballot: annual meeting

*What members vote on at each annual meeting. The steps before and after the vote are policy, in [`policies/how-we-decide.md`](policies/how-we-decide.md).*

**Annual meeting:** the second Thursday of every April, 6 to 8 pm, at 1404 Grand Ave, Unit B, Columbia. There is no Open Hack Night that evening.

**Voting:** the Secretary opens voting during the meeting, after the discussion, and it stays open for 8 days, until about 7 pm on the Friday of the following week (the ballot email gives the exact time). Each voter gets a personal ballot link by email.

**Who votes:** full members (Standard or Supporter dues) in good standing who have been members for at least 30 days when voting opens, so who joined at least 30 days before the annual meeting (bylaws 5.5). Hardship and Legacy members don't vote (bylaws 2.1). Only voters get the notice and the ballot.

**Quorum:** ballots from at least 10% of the full members on the voter roll, and no fewer than 5.

## The questions

| # | Question | Kind | Passes with |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Each proposed bylaw change, as tagged `ballot/<yyyy-mm-dd>` when voting opens. At the first annual meeting, a single question: do you ratify the bylaws in [`bylaws.md`](bylaws.md)? | Binding | Two-thirds of votes cast |
| 2 | Elect the President, Vice President, Secretary, Treasurer, and Membership Director | Binding, one race per seat. At the first annual meeting, counts only if the bylaws are ratified (bylaws 5.11) | Most votes, ties broken by the President (bylaws 5.9); a lone candidate needs a majority of yes votes |

Abstentions count toward quorum but not as votes cast. If the bylaws are not ratified at the first annual meeting, the current officers stay in office and the board brings revised bylaws to the members within 120 days.
