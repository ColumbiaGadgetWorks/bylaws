# Membership and access

*Policy draft. Not adopted.*

## Joining

1. **Sign up online** at columbiagadgetworks.org/membership/join/. You'll sign the waiver and the member agreement and upload a photo of your ID.
2. **Pay dues**, or be approved for a hardship waiver.
3. **Complete the safety orientation** (about 20 minutes) with a director or Zone Boss. Then you get your badge.

Within your first month, a director or the Membership Lead checks in with you.

## Age

| Age | Rules |
|---|---|
| 18 and over | Full membership |
| 16 and 17 | May be a member if a parent or guardian also signs the waiver and the member agreement |
| Under 16 | May attend Open Hack Night and classes only with a parent or guardian present the whole time. The parent or guardian signs the waiver too |

Board seats are open only to members 18 or older.

## Dues

| Type | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | $50 a month | May be paid quarterly |
| Supporter | $100 a month | Same as Standard in every way. The extra $50 is a tax-deductible donation |
| Hardship | $0 | Apply privately to the board. Lasts 12 months and can be renewed. No more than [20% of members, or 4, whichever is more] |
| Legacy | $40 a month | Only for members already paying $40. Ends when the new bylaws are adopted |

Every type has the same rights, including the vote. Who holds a hardship waiver is kept confidential.

## Missed dues

- If a payment fails or is cancelled, Dolibarr sends reminder emails after 3, 7, and 14 days.
- If dues are still unpaid 30 days after the missed payment, membership ends and the badge is turned off. Time you've already paid for always runs out first.
- You can rejoin any time. No back dues.

## Access

- **Your badge is yours.** Don't lend it. If you let someone in, stay with them.
- **Guests.** Any member may bring a guest. The guest signs the waiver, and the member stays with them the whole time.
- **Open Hack Night**, Thursdays 6 to 8 pm, is free and open to everyone. Every visitor signs the waiver.
- **Checkout-gated tools** need a recorded checkout before anyone uses them, member or visitor.
