# Storage and donations

*Policy draft. Not adopted.*

## Storage

- Every member gets one labeled bin, included in dues. If the lid won't close, it's too full.
- An active project in a zone needs a tag with your name and the date. The Zone Boss sets how long it may stay.
- Anything unlabeled is moved to the storage area, and whoever moves it posts a photo on Discord. After one week it goes to the free shelf.
- Never store anything in a walkway, in front of an electrical panel, or inside a machine's working area.
- A member who leaves has 30 days to clear out their things.

## Donations

- **Ask before you bring it.** Anything left without approval is thrown away.
- The donor names which Zone Boss gets first refusal, and "no thanks" is a complete answer. If no zone is named and the Zone Bosses disagree, the board decides.
- Anything no zone takes goes on the donation shelf. The shelf has a fixed footprint and one director in charge of it. Each item gets a dated dot, and anything still there after a year is removed.
