# Zones and roles

*Policy draft. Not adopted. The board decides* what *the shop is; a Zone Boss decides* how *their zone works.*

## Zones

There are six zones: Woodworking, Machining, Metalworking, Electronics, Digital Fabrication, and Crafts.

The classroom, kitchen, entrance, donation shelf, and Open Hack Night are not part of any zone. The board, or a Lead the board names, runs them, and the general fund pays for them.

## Roles

| Role | How appointed | Term |
|---|---|---|
| **Zone Boss** | By the board, one per zone; reports to the Shop Steward | Ends 30 April. Every role is reappointed together each year |
| **Lead** | By the board, for a job that isn't a place (membership, classes, systems…) | Ends 30 April |
| **Deputy** | By a Zone Boss or Lead, no board approval | Until the person who appointed them says otherwise |

- Anyone in these roles may resign at any time. The board may remove anyone in these roles at any time by majority vote, and records the reason in the minutes.
- **Two-roles rule.** No one holds more than two of: board seat, Zone Boss, Lead.
- A director who is a Zone Boss does not vote on their own zone's budget or appointment.

## What a Zone Boss decides alone

- The layout and organization of the zone, and extra storage in it (including any fee)
- How long tagged projects may stay in the zone
- Who may teach checkouts; each class's price tier; tool rental rates
- Materials policy, cleanup standard, and which donations the zone accepts
- Who pays for damage in the zone
- Spending within the zone balance and the limits in `money.md`

## What the board decides

- How much floor space each zone gets
- Which tools are checkout-gated (the Zone Boss proposes)
- Anything that affects the building, electrical, dust collection, or another zone
- Contracts, ongoing costs, and spending above the limits
- Membership, dues, and discipline

## What a Zone Boss owes the shop

- Keep the zone's wiki page current.
- Walk and tidy the zone every month.
- Run at least one class a quarter.
- Answer member requests within a week, even if the answer is no.
- Before each quarterly board meeting, post five lines on the zone's wiki page: what changed, what's broken, spending and balance, classes, and what the zone needs from the board.
