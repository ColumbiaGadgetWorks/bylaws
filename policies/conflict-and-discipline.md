# Conflict and discipline

*Policy draft. Not adopted.*

## Disagreements between members

1. **Talk directly first**, if it's safe to do so.
2. **Ask a director** who isn't involved to help, if that doesn't work. They mediate and write down the outcome.
3. **The board decides** if mediation fails.

**Harassment, threats, and safety problems skip steps 1 and 2.** Report them straight to any director.

## What the board can do

Depending on what happened, the board may:
- give a warning;
- limit someone's access for a set time;
- suspend someone's access immediately (bylaws 2.5);
- suspend or expel a member, with the notice and hearing required by bylaws 2.4.

A director who is involved in the matter takes no part in the decision. Decisions are recorded in closed-session minutes.

## Zone disputes

| Situation | Who decides |
|---|---|
| A member disagrees with a zone rule | The Zone Boss decides and owes the member one reason |
| A member thinks a Zone Boss acted outside their authority | The board, on the question of authority only |
| Two Zone Bosses disagree | They talk first. After two weeks, either may take it to the board |
