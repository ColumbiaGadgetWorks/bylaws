COLUMBIA GADGET WORKS
MEMBER AGREEMENT

This agreement is between me and Columbia Gadget Works ("CGW"). It summarizes CGW's bylaws and policies, which are published at columbiagadgetworks.org/policies/. Where this summary and the published bylaws and policies differ, the published version controls.

1. BYLAWS AND POLICIES

I agree to follow CGW's bylaws and policies, and the zone rules each Zone Boss posts on the zone's wiki page. The board may change the policies at any time by a vote of at least three directors, and a change takes effect when it is published. Only the members can change the bylaws.

2. BECOMING A MEMBER

I become a member once I have signed the waiver and this agreement and my dues are current, or the board has approved a hardship waiver for me. I get my badge after I complete the safety orientation, about 20 minutes with a director or Zone Boss.

Members are 18 or older. A person aged 16 or 17 may be a member if a parent or guardian also signs the waiver and this agreement.

3. DUES

- My dues depend on my membership type: Standard ($50 a month, or quarterly), Supporter ($100 a month, of which $50 is a tax-deductible donation), Hardship ($0, approved by the board for 12 months at a time), or Legacy ($40 a month, for members already paying $40, until the new bylaws are adopted). Standard and Supporter members are full members and vote. Hardship and Legacy members do not vote or hold a board seat, but otherwise have the same rights and use of the space.
- If a payment fails or is cancelled, CGW sends me reminder emails. If my dues are still unpaid 30 days after the missed payment, my membership ends and my badge is turned off. Until then, if I am a full member, I keep my vote. Time I have already paid for always runs out first.
- I can cancel at any time, and I can rejoin later without paying back dues.

4. MY BADGE

My badge is mine. I will not lend it. If I let someone in, I stay with them. I will return any physical key, card, or fob when my membership ends.

5. GUESTS AND VISITORS

I may bring a guest. My guest signs the waiver before entering the work area, and I stay with them the whole time. Open Hack Night, Thursdays from 6 to 8 pm, is free and open to everyone, and every visitor signs the waiver. A child under 16 may attend Open Hack Night and classes only with a parent or guardian present the whole time.

6. SAFETY

- I use only the tools I am trained on, and a checkout-gated tool only once I have a recorded checkout on it.
- I wear eye protection around machines, and I do not operate anything while impaired by alcohol or drugs.
- Anyone may stop unsafe work on the spot, and I stop when asked. A Zone Boss or director may bar me from a tool for the rest of the day; any longer bar is the board's decision.
- I report any injury or near-miss to a director within 24 hours, and any breakage to the Zone Boss or on Discord the same day. Reporting damage never gets anyone in trouble; hiding it does.
- The Zone Boss decides who pays for damage in their zone.

7. CONDUCT

I am welcoming, respectful, and considerate. I clean up after myself, put tools back, and do not hog a machine. I do not campaign for candidates or causes, or proselytize, in the shop. I do not harass, discriminate against, threaten, or intimidate anyone, in the space, at CGW events, or on CGW's online channels, and I stop immediately if asked to stop a behavior. I can raise a problem with any director or Zone Boss, or at mediation@columbiagadgetworks.org.

8. ZONES

Each zone is run by a Zone Boss. If I disagree with a zone rule, the Zone Boss decides and owes me one reason. If I think a Zone Boss acted outside their authority, I can ask the board to decide that question.

9. CONFLICTS AND DISCIPLINE

- If I have a disagreement with another member, I first try to talk it through directly, where that is safe. If that fails, I ask a director who is not involved to help, and if that fails, the board decides.
- Harassment, threats, and safety problems go straight to any director.
- The board may warn me or limit my access for up to 30 days. When safety, property, or a person is at risk, any two directors may suspend my access immediately; the suspension ends after 14 days unless the board confirms it.
- I can be suspended or expelled as a member only after written notice with the reasons at least 15 days beforehand, a chance to be heard by the board at least 5 days before it takes effect, and a vote of two-thirds of the directors eligible to vote on it.

10. STORAGE AND DONATIONS

- I get one labeled bin, included in my dues. If the lid won't close, it is too full.
- An active project I leave in a zone carries a tag with my name and the date, for as long as the Zone Boss allows.
- Anything unlabeled is moved to the storage area with a photo posted on Discord, and goes to the free shelf after one week.
- I never store anything in a walkway, in front of an electrical panel, or inside a machine's working area.
- When my membership ends, I have 30 days to clear out my things.
- I ask before bringing a donation. Anything left without approval is thrown away.

11. RECORDS AND COMMUNICATION

CGW keeps my name, contact details, signed documents, photo ID, and payment history in its membership records, uses them to run my membership, and does not sell them. Only directors can see my ID photo, and it is deleted 30 days after my membership ends. Whether I hold a hardship waiver is kept confidential.

I agree to receive official CGW notices and ballots by email, including notices of member votes and of missed dues, at the address on my member record, and I will keep that address current.

12. NO OWNERSHIP

Membership gives me the use of the space under these rules, and, as a full member, a vote. It gives me no ownership of CGW or its property, and it cannot be transferred. I may resign at any time.

By signing, I confirm that I have read this agreement and agree to follow it.
