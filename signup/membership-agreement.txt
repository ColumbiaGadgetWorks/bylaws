COLUMBIA GADGET WORKS
MEMBERSHIP AGREEMENT

This agreement is between me and Columbia Gadget Works ("CGW"). The rules below summarize CGW's Bylaws and Policies, which are posted on the wiki. Where this summary and the posted Bylaws and Policies differ, the posted version controls.

1. BYLAWS AND POLICIES

I agree to follow CGW's Bylaws and Policies, and the zone rules posted by each Zone Boss. The board may change the policies by majority vote, and a change takes effect when it is posted, dated, on the wiki. Only the members can change the bylaws.

2. BECOMING A MEMBER

I become a member once I have a signed liability waiver on file, have completed the safety orientation, and have paid my dues or been approved for a scholarship. I get no door code until I have completed orientation. The minimum age is 16, and a member under 18 needs a parent or guardian to sign the waiver.

3. DUES

- Dues cover a calendar month. My dues are set by my membership type (Standard, Supporter, Scholarship, or Legacy), and every type has the same rights, including the vote.
- If a month is still unpaid on the 16th of the following month, my door access is turned off.
- My membership ends 30 days after CGW emails me written notice of unpaid dues, unless I pay.
- I can cancel at any time. Rejoining later does not require back-dues.

4. DOOR ACCESS

My door code or credential is personal to me. I will not share it. If I let someone in and leave them unattended, I lose access for two weeks. I will return any physical key, card, or fob when my membership ends.

5. GUESTS AND VISITORS

Open Hack Night is free and open to the public on Thursdays at 6 pm. At any other time, a guest may be in the space only while I host them. I stay with my guest the whole time, I am responsible for them, and they sign the waiver before entering the work area. A guest under 16 needs a parent or guardian present.

6. SAFETY

- I use a checkout-gated tool only after a recorded checkout.
- I do not use tools while impaired by alcohol or drugs.
- Anyone may stop unsafe work on the spot, and I will stop when asked. A Zone Boss or board member may bar me from a tool for the rest of the day.
- I report injuries and near-misses to the board within 24 hours, and breakage to the Zone Boss or on Discord the same day. Reporting is never punished; hiding damage is.
- A Zone Boss may decide that I pay for damage I cause in their zone.

7. CONDUCT

I treat people with respect. I do not harass, threaten, or discriminate against anyone on the basis of any personal characteristic, in the space, at CGW events, or on CGW's online channels. I clean up my mess, and I keep politics and religion out of the shop.

8. ZONES

Each zone is run by a Zone Boss, who decides how that zone works. If I disagree with a zone rule, I take it to the Zone Boss, whose decision stands. If I think a Zone Boss has acted outside their authority, I can ask the board to rule on that question.

9. CONFLICTS AND DISCIPLINE

- If I have a conflict with another member, I first try to talk it through directly, where that is safe. If that fails, I take it to one of the two Peace Officers named on the wiki, and if mediation fails, the board decides.
- Harassment, threats, and safety issues go straight to any board member or Peace Officer.
- The board may warn me, restrict my access for a set time, or suspend my access. Two board members may suspend my access immediately when there is a risk to safety, property, or a person, and the board must confirm it within 14 days.
- I can be expelled only after 15 days' written notice with the reasons, a chance to be heard by the board, and a two-thirds vote of the board.

10. STORAGE AND DONATIONS

- I get one labeled storage bin, included in my dues, and its lid must close.
- Projects I leave in a zone carry my name and the date, for as long as the Zone Boss allows.
- Unlabeled items are moved to storage with a photo posted on Discord, and go to the free shelf after one week.
- I keep walkways, electrical panels, and machine working areas clear.
- When my membership ends, I have 30 days to remove my belongings.
- I offer donations to a Zone Boss before bringing them in. Items dropped off unannounced are thrown away.

11. RECORDS AND COMMUNICATION

CGW keeps my name, contact details, signed documents, photo ID, and payment history in its membership records, uses them to run my membership, and does not sell them. CGW sends official notices, including notices of member meetings and of unpaid dues, by email to the address it has for me, and I will keep that address current.

12. NO OWNERSHIP

Membership gives me a vote and the use of the space under these rules. It gives me no ownership of CGW or its property, and it cannot be transferred. I may resign at any time.

By signing, I confirm that I have read this agreement and agree to follow it.
